Standard and daylight names

Where clocks change for summer, the zone has two names. In North America the S becomes a D: Eastern Standard Time (EST) in winter and Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) in summer. People often say "EST" all year, which is wrong by an hour in summer. Writing "ET" or "Eastern Time" avoids the mistake.

Abbreviations with more than one meaning

Abbreviations are not official and several clash:

  • IST: India Standard Time (UTC+5:30), Irish Standard Time (UTC+1) and Israel Standard Time (UTC+2).
  • CST: Central Standard Time in North America (UTC−6), China Standard Time (UTC+8) and Cuba Standard Time (UTC−5).
  • BST: British Summer Time (UTC+1) and Bangladesh Standard Time (UTC+6).
  • AST: Atlantic Standard Time (UTC−4) and Arabia Standard Time (UTC+3).

When it matters, give the UTC offset or the city: "3 pm IST (UTC+5:30)" or "3 pm Mumbai time". The converter works with cities, so it never mixes these up.

GMT or UTC?

For everyday use they show the same time. GMT is the name of a time zone, used in the United Kingdom in winter. UTC is the modern scientific standard kept by atomic clocks, and is not a time zone of any country.
