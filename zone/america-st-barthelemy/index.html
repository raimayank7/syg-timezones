About this time zone

All of Saint Barthelemy keeps one time. Computers and phones know it by the zone name America/St_Barthelemy. Its time is UTC−4, which means it is 4 hours behind Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) all year round. The short name used for this time is AST. Compared with India (IST), it is 9 hours 30 minutes behind India.

Clock changes in 2026 and 2027

Saint Barthelemy does not change its clocks in 2026 or 2027. The time stays at UTC−4 all year, so there is no daylight saving time to remember.
