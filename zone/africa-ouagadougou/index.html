About this time zone

All of Burkina Faso keeps one time. Computers and phones know it by the zone name Africa/Ouagadougou. Its time is UTC, which means it is the same as Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) all year round. The short name used for this time is GMT. Compared with India (IST), it is 5 hours 30 minutes behind India.

Clock changes in 2026 and 2027

Burkina Faso does not change its clocks in 2026 or 2027. The time stays at UTC all year, so there is no daylight saving time to remember.

Clock time and sun time in Burkina Faso

A time zone is an agreement, and the sun does not follow it exactly. Ouagadougou, the largest city in this zone, lies at about 1.5° west. Each degree of longitude is worth four minutes, so by the sun alone its time would be about 6 minutes behind UTC. The clock is set to UTC. The sun is therefore highest in the sky at about 12:06 pm by the clock, not at 12:00. That is a close match: clock and sun agree to within a quarter of an hour. Other places in the zone, to the east or west of Ouagadougou, see the sun peak a little earlier or later. Through the year the figure also drifts by up to a quarter of an hour either way.

How long the day is

Ouagadougou is about 12.4° north of the equator. On the longest day of the year, around 21 June, the sun is up for about 12 hours 51 minutes. On the shortest, around 21 December, for about 11 hours 24 minutes. The difference between summer and winter is about 1 hour 27 minutes, and the clocks stay the same all year.

Office hours shared with other places

If both sides work from 9 am to 6 pm, these are the hours when a call falls inside the working day at both ends. Times are for Burkina Faso.

  • With India, all year: 9:00 am to 12:30 pm here, which is 2:30 pm to 6:00 pm in India (3 hours 30 minutes shared).
  • With London, in January: 9:00 am to 6:00 pm here, which is 9:00 am to 6:00 pm in London (9 hours shared). In July: 9:00 am to 5:00 pm here, which is 10:00 am to 6:00 pm in London (8 hours shared).
  • With New York, in January: 2:00 pm to 6:00 pm here, which is 9:00 am to 1:00 pm in New York (4 hours shared). In July: 1:00 pm to 6:00 pm here, which is 9:00 am to 2:00 pm in New York (5 hours shared).

How many people keep this time

The GeoNames list has 74 towns and cities of 15,000 people or more in this zone area, with about 6.1 million people between them. People in villages and smaller towns are not in that count, so the true number on this clock is higher.

Cities that keep this time

The largest cities in this zone: Ouagadougou, Bobo-Dioulasso, Koudougou, Saaba, Ouahigouya, Kaya, Banfora, Pouytenga, Houndé, Fada N'gourma, Nioko I, Dédougou, Tenkodogo, Djibo.

Places with exactly the same time

These zones show the same time as Burkina Faso through the whole year: Abidjan, Ivory Coast, Accra, Ghana, Bamako, Mali, Banjul, Gambia, Bissau, Guinea-Bissau, Conakry, Guinea, Dakar, Senegal, Freetown, Sierra Leone, Lome, Togo, Monrovia, Liberia, Nouakchott, Mauritania, Sao Tome, Sao Tome and Principe, Reykjavik, Iceland, St Helena, Saint Helena.
