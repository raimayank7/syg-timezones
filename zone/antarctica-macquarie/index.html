About this time zone

Macquarie is one of the 12 time zone areas of Australia. Computers and phones know it by the zone name Antarctica/Macquarie. Its time is UTC+11 in January and UTC+10 in July, because the clocks are changed during the year. The short names used for this time are AEDT and AEST. Compared with India (IST), it is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of India in January and 4 hours 30 minutes ahead of India in July.
