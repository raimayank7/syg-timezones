About this time zone

All of Andorra keeps one time. Computers and phones know it by the zone name Europe/Andorra. Its time is UTC+1 in January and UTC+2 in July, because the clocks are changed during the year. The short names used for this time are CET and CEST. Compared with India (IST), it is 4 hours 30 minutes behind India in January and 3 hours 30 minutes behind India in July.

Counting from those dates, the clocks in Andorra stay forward for 210 days, from 29 March 2026 to 25 October 2026. That is about 30 weeks, or 58% of a year, on UTC+2 and the rest on UTC+1.

Clock time and sun time in Andorra

A time zone is an agreement, and the sun does not follow it exactly. Andorra la Vella, the largest city in this zone, lies at about 1.5° east. Each degree of longitude is worth four minutes, so by the sun alone its time would be about 6 minutes ahead of UTC. The clock is set to UTC+1. The sun is therefore highest in the sky at about 12:54 pm by the clock on standard time, and at about 1:54 pm while the clocks are moved (UTC+2). The clock runs ahead of the sun here, which means later sunrises and lighter evenings than the map alone would give. Other places in the zone, to the east or west of Andorra la Vella, see the sun peak a little earlier or later. Through the year the figure also drifts by up to a quarter of an hour either way.

How long the day is

Andorra la Vella is about 42.5° north of the equator. On the longest day of the year, around 21 June, the sun is up for about 15 hours 18 minutes. On the shortest, around 21 December, for about 9 hours 3 minutes. That swing of about 6 hours 15 minutes between summer and winter is the reason clocks are moved here: the extra summer daylight is shifted from the early morning to the evening.

Office hours shared with other places

If both sides work from 9 am to 6 pm, these are the hours when a call falls inside the working day at both ends. Times are for Andorra.

  • With India, in January: 9:00 am to 1:30 pm here, which is 1:30 pm to 6:00 pm in India (4 hours 30 minutes shared). In July: 9:00 am to 2:30 pm here, which is 12:30 pm to 6:00 pm in India (5 hours 30 minutes shared).
  • With London, all year: 10:00 am to 6:00 pm here, which is 9:00 am to 5:00 pm in London (8 hours shared).
  • With New York, all year: 3:00 pm to 6:00 pm here, which is 9:00 am to 12:00 pm in New York (3 hours shared).

How many people keep this time

The GeoNames list has 2 towns and cities of 15,000 people or more in this zone area, with about 36,000 people between them. People in villages and smaller towns are not in that count, so the true number on this clock is higher.
