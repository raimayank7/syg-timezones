About this time zone

Eucla is one of the 12 time zone areas of Australia. Computers and phones know it by the zone name Australia/Eucla. Its time is UTC+8:45, which means it is 8 hours 45 minutes ahead of Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) all year round. Compared with India (IST), it is 3 hours 15 minutes ahead of India.

Clock changes in 2026 and 2027

Eucla does not change its clocks in 2026 or 2027. The time stays at UTC+8:45 all year, so there is no daylight saving time to remember.

Office hours shared with other places

If both sides work from 9 am to 6 pm, these are the hours when a call falls inside the working day at both ends. Times are for Eucla.

  • With India, all year: 12:15 pm to 6:00 pm here, which is 9:00 am to 2:45 pm in India (5 hours 45 minutes shared).
  • With London, in January: 5:45 pm to 6:00 pm here, which is 9:00 am to 9:15 am in London (15 minutes shared). In July: 4:45 pm to 6:00 pm here, which is 9:00 am to 10:15 am in London (1 hour 15 minutes shared).
  • With New York, in January: no shared office hour: 9:00 am here is 7:15 pm in New York, and 6:00 pm here is 4:15 am there, so one side has to start early or stay late. In July: no shared office hour: 9:00 am here is 8:15 pm in New York, and 6:00 pm here is 5:15 am there, so one side has to start early or stay late.
