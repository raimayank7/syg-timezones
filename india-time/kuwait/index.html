The time difference

Kuwait is 2 hours 30 minutes behind India all year. Neither place changes its clocks, so the difference never moves. India is always UTC+5:30, known as IST.

Good hours to start a call (8 am to 9 pm in both places): from 11:00 am to 8:00 pm India time. Hours that start inside office time (9 am to 6 pm) in both: 12:00 pm to 5:00 pm India time.

Worked examples

All year. An office that closes at 6:00 pm in India closes when it is 3:30 pm in Kuwait. A 9:00 am start in Kuwait is 11:30 am in India, and its 6:00 pm finish is 8:30 pm in India. A message sent at 10:30 pm from India lands at 8:00 pm in Kuwait.

When the date is different

Kuwait is behind India, so for 2 hours 30 minutes of every day the calendar date differs: from midnight until 2:30 am India time, it is still the previous day in Kuwait. Mention the date as well as the time when you fix something in those hours.

Why the gap is what it is

Indian Standard Time follows the sun at longitude 82.5° east. The zone that Kuwait belongs to has its largest city, Al Aḩmadī, at about 48.1° east. The sun crosses the sky between those two lines in about 2 hours 18 minutes. Clock time rounds that to a tidy figure: 2 hours 30 minutes. The two figures differ because every country picks a round offset that suits its whole territory, and some add summer time on top.
