The time difference

Kuala Lumpur is 2 hours 30 minutes ahead of India all year. Neither place changes its clocks, so the difference never moves. India is always UTC+5:30, known as IST.

Worked examples

All year. An office that closes at 6:00 pm in India closes when it is 8:30 pm in Kuala Lumpur. A 9:00 am start in Kuala Lumpur is 6:30 am in India, and its 6:00 pm finish is 3:30 pm in India. A message sent at 10:30 pm from India lands at 1:00 am the next day in Kuala Lumpur.

When the date is different

Kuala Lumpur is ahead of India, so for 2 hours 30 minutes of every day the calendar date differs: from 9:30 pm India time until midnight, Kuala Lumpur has already moved to the next day.

Why the gap is what it is

Indian Standard Time follows the sun at longitude 82.5° east. The zone that Kuala Lumpur belongs to has its largest city, Kuala Lumpur, at about 101.7° east. The sun crosses the sky between those two lines in about 1 hour 17 minutes. Clock time rounds that to a tidy figure: 2 hours 30 minutes. The two figures differ because every country picks a round offset that suits its whole territory, and some add summer time on top.
