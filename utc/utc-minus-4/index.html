UTC−4 time now

Places that keep the time UTC−4, which is 4 hours behind Coordinated Universal Time.

--:--

The time at UTC−4 right now, from your device's clock.

What UTC−4 means

A clock set to UTC−4 shows UTC minus 4 hours. When it is 12:00 noon UTC, it is 8:00 am here. Compared with India (UTC+5:30) it is 9 hours 30 minutes behind.

Largest cities on this time

New York City, Santiago, Caracas, Toronto, Brooklyn, Queens, Manaus, Santo Domingo.
