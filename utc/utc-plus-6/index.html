UTC+6 time now

Places that keep the time UTC+6, which is 6 hours ahead of Coordinated Universal Time.

--:--

The time at UTC+6 right now, from your device's clock.

What UTC+6 means

A clock set to UTC+6 shows UTC plus 6 hours. When it is 12:00 noon UTC, it is 6:00 pm here. Compared with India (UTC+5:30) it is 30 minutes ahead.

Converting to and from UTC+6

To turn UTC into this time, add 6 hours. To go back to UTC, subtract the same amount. Three examples: 6:00 am UTC is 12:00 pm here; 3:00 pm UTC is 9:00 pm here; 9:00 am here is 3:00 am UTC. The calendar date changes whenever the sum passes midnight.

Where UTC+6 sits on the map

The Earth turns 15 degrees of longitude every hour. The line where the sun-time matches UTC+6 exactly is 90° east. The places that use it are spread much wider than that one line. Of the main cities listed below, Omsk (73.4° east) is the farthest west and Dhaka (90.4° east) the farthest east. By the sun they are about 1 hour 8 minutes apart, yet their clocks agree.

Who is on UTC+6, and when

5 zone areas in 5 countries and territories keep UTC+6 through the whole year. The towns of 15,000 or more there hold about 52 million people (GeoNames figures). Nobody moves on to or off this offset during the year, so the list below is the same in every season.

Noon at UTC+6 elsewhere

When a clock on UTC+6 shows 12:00 noon, it is 11:30 am in India; 6:00 am in London in January and 7:00 am in July; 1:00 am in New York in January and 2:00 am in July; 3:00 pm in Tokyo. For any other place or date, use the converter.

Largest cities on this time

Dhaka, Ürümqi, Chattogram, Gazipur, Omsk, Bishkek, Shihezi, Korla.
