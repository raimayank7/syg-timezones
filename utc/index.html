What is a UTC offset?

UTC, Coordinated Universal Time, is the reference clock of the world. A UTC offset says how far a place's clock is from it. India is UTC+5:30: five and a half hours ahead. New York in winter is UTC−5: five hours behind. To convert between two places, subtract one offset from the other.

Half and quarter hours

Most offsets are whole hours, but not all. India, Sri Lanka, Iran, Afghanistan, Myanmar and parts of Australia use a half hour. Nepal (UTC+5:45) and the Chatham Islands of New Zealand use a quarter hour.

Why the range is more than 24 hours

Offsets run from UTC−11 to UTC+14, a spread of 25 hours or more. Some Pacific island nations chose large positive offsets so that the whole country shares one calendar date. As a result, three different dates can exist on Earth at the same moment for a short time each day.
