UTC−5 time now

Places that keep the time UTC−5, which is 5 hours behind Coordinated Universal Time.

--:--

The time at UTC−5 right now, from your device's clock.

What UTC−5 means

A clock set to UTC−5 shows UTC minus 5 hours. When it is 12:00 noon UTC, it is 7:00 am here. Compared with India (UTC+5:30) it is 10 hours 30 minutes behind.

Converting to and from UTC−5

To turn UTC into this time, subtract 5 hours. To go back to UTC, add the same amount. Three examples: 6:00 am UTC is 1:00 am here; 3:00 pm UTC is 10:00 am here; 9:00 am here is 2:00 pm UTC. The calendar date changes whenever the sum passes midnight.

Where UTC−5 sits on the map

The Earth turns 15 degrees of longitude every hour. The line where the sun-time matches UTC−5 exactly is 75° west. The places that use it are spread much wider than that one line. Of the main cities listed below, Ojinaga (104.5° west) is the farthest west and Rio Branco (67.8° west) the farthest east. By the sun they are about 2 hours 27 minutes apart, yet their clocks agree.

Who is on UTC−5, and when

9 zone areas in 8 countries and territories keep UTC−5 through the whole year. The towns of 15,000 or more there hold about 74 million people (GeoNames figures). 15 more zone areas are on it for only part of the year: for 10 of them it is the standard (winter) time and for 5 it is the moved (summer) time. That is why the number of people on UTC−5 rises and falls with the seasons.

Noon at UTC−5 elsewhere

When a clock on UTC−5 shows 12:00 noon, it is 10:30 pm in India; 5:00 pm in London in January and 6:00 pm in July; 12:00 pm in New York in January and 1:00 pm in July; 2:00 am the next day in Tokyo. For any other place or date, use the converter.
