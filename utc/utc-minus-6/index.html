UTC−6 time now

Places that keep the time UTC−6, which is 6 hours behind Coordinated Universal Time.

--:--

The time at UTC−6 right now, from your device's clock.

What UTC−6 means

A clock set to UTC−6 shows UTC minus 6 hours. When it is 12:00 noon UTC, it is 6:00 am here. Compared with India (UTC+5:30) it is 11 hours 30 minutes behind.

Converting to and from UTC−6

To turn UTC into this time, subtract 6 hours. To go back to UTC, add the same amount. Three examples: 6:00 am UTC is 12:00 am here; 3:00 pm UTC is 9:00 am here; 9:00 am here is 3:00 pm UTC. The calendar date changes whenever the sum passes midnight.

Where UTC−6 sits on the map

The Earth turns 15 degrees of longitude every hour. The line where the sun-time matches UTC−6 exactly is 90° west. The places that use it are spread much wider than that one line. Of the main cities listed below, Boise (116.2° west) is the farthest west and San José (84.1° west) the farthest east. By the sun they are about 2 hours 8 minutes apart, yet their clocks agree.

Who is on UTC−6, and when

13 zone areas in 8 countries and territories keep UTC−6 through the whole year. The towns of 15,000 or more there hold about 94 million people (GeoNames figures). 9 more zone areas are on it for only part of the year: for 5 of them it is the standard (winter) time and for 4 it is the moved (summer) time. That is why the number of people on UTC−6 rises and falls with the seasons.

Noon at UTC−6 elsewhere

When a clock on UTC−6 shows 12:00 noon, it is 11:30 pm in India; 6:00 pm in London in January and 7:00 pm in July; 1:00 pm in New York in January and 2:00 pm in July; 3:00 am the next day in Tokyo. For any other place or date, use the converter.
